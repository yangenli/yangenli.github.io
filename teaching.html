In the Classroom

Real data, real decisions

My courses are built around applying theory to live markets. Students construct and manage their own portfolios, watch diversification and risk-adjusted performance play out against current market data, and connect every metric — expected return, volatility, beta, the Sharpe and Treynor measures, alpha — to positions they chose themselves.

I bring my research toolkit into teaching: a custom-built interactive portfolio tracker, Python demonstrations of the tools professionals actually use, and open-book assessments that reward understanding over memorization.
