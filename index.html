Job Market Paper

Future Proves Past: Detecting Hidden Fraud under Imperfect Enforcement Labels

SEC enforcement records miss most fraud, so a fraud detector should not be graded on them. We train a model with help from future accounting outcomes and evaluate it entirely outside the enforcement label. It reaches fraud the SEC never surfaces.

with C. Wei Li, Yuxuan Li, and Anand M. Vijh
